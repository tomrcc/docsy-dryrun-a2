This is placeholder documentation. It explains how to edit this site in
CloudCannon, so keep it until your team knows its way around, then replace it
with your own.

{{% /pageinfo %}}

The **Docs** collection holds every page in this section. Each folder is a
section with its own landing page (`_index.md`), and each markdown file is a
page in the left-hand navigation.

- Pages are ordered by their **Weight**: lower numbers come first.
- A page's **Link title** is the shorter name used in the navigation.
- The **Description** appears under the page title and in search results.
