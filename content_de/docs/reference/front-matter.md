## Docs pages

| Field           | What it does                                                        |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Title**       | The page heading and the browser tab title                          |
| **Link title**  | A shorter title for the navigation and breadcrumbs (optional)       |
| **Description** | The lead paragraph under the title, and the search-result summary   |
| **Weight**      | The page's position in the navigation — lower numbers come first    |
| **Tags**, **Categories** | Group pages on the tag and category pages              |
| **Draft**       | Draft pages aren't published, and can't be previewed in the Visual Editor |

## Blog posts

Blog posts have the same fields, plus **Date** (posts are listed newest first)
and **Author**.

## Landing pages

Landing pages have **Content blocks** instead of a markdown body. Each block
has its own fields; hover over a field for a description.
